Product Deep Dive — We Truly Understand This Product

✅ 优势 / 机会点
✅ Strengths / Opportunities
  • 品类空白赛道：海外无直接竞品，轻恐社交玩法尚未有成熟产品占据，先发可建立品类心智 但需承担"教育市场"的成本和时间代价
  • 女性用户高占比：女性向社交恐怖游戏是被严重低估的细分市场，该用户群付费意愿强、社交裂变能力强 与主流恐怖游戏以男性为主的用户结构形成差异化
  • 拉美天然适配：巴西、墨西哥用户偏好派对式、轻社交的多人玩法；超自然行动的"和朋友一起玩轻恐"定位高度契合 拉美下载量基数大，适合冲量
  • 巨人海外独立分支：巨人已有海外版本独立运营经验，承诺会针对海外做本地化优化，而不是简单搬运国内版本 具体本地化方案尚未披露，待进一步了解
  • Category blue ocean: No direct overseas competitor; social light-horror has no established player overseas — first-mover can own category mindshare But must bear market education costs and time investment
  • High female user ratio: Female-oriented social horror is severely underserved; this audience has strong spending intent and social virality Clear differentiation from male-dominated mainstream horror games
  • Natural LATAM fit: Brazil and Mexico users favor party-style, light social multiplayer; "play light horror with friends" positioning is a strong match Large LATAM download base, good for volume
  • Giant overseas branch: Giant has separate overseas operations experience, committed to localization rather than simply porting the domestic version Specific localization roadmap not yet disclosed — needs follow-up
⚠️ 核心问题（基于实际体验）
⚠️ Core Issues (Based on Hands-on Experience)
  • 早期封锁好友组队 — 最大blocke：新玩家需要打满若干段位赛才能解锁好友组队功能，但产品的核心卖点就是"和朋友一起玩"，这造成了严重的逻辑矛盾 建议：3–5局新手引导后立即开放组队；否则第一批用户会在最关键的社交链形成期大量流失
  • 角色中国脸 + 代入感问题：现有角色设计以中国人面孔为主，海外玩家——尤其是东南亚和拉美用户——代入感弱，影响情感连接 核心决策：① 做本地化英雄（针对各市场设计本土角色）② 更换为中性国际化风格角色；两个方案成本和周期差异很大
  • 盗墓/中式民俗题材接受度存疑：盗墓文化和中国民俗元素在海外几乎没有认知基础，可能被认为猎奇而非恐怖，题材吸引力难以判断 可能需要在营销层面"去中国化"，重新包装成更通用的"超自然解谜"概念
  • 玩法深度偏浅，留存钩子不足：亲自体验后感受明显：游戏内容单薄，核心玩法loop重复性高，缺乏支撑长线留存的成长体系和策略深度 对比第五人格：第五人格有完整的角色机制、赛季系统和深度技巧曲线；超自然行动当前版本差距明显
  • 传播链弱，冷启动难：玩法本身不具备自发病毒传播特性，无"高光时刻"；早期需要高度依赖付费买量，自然增长有限 预计用户获取成本偏高，ROI压力较大
  • No early friend-team play — biggest blocker: New players must complete multiple ranked matches before unlocking friend-team functionality, but the core pitch IS "play with friends" — fundamental contradiction Recommendation: Unlock teaming after 3–5 onboarding matches; otherwise critical early social chain formation collapses
  • Chinese-face characters + immersion gap: Current characters are predominantly Chinese-featured; SEA and LATAM players struggle to identify with them, weakening emotional connection Core decision: ① Localized heroes (region-specific characters) ② Replace with culturally neutral / international style — vastly different cost and timeline
  • Tomb-raider / Chinese folklore theme — uncertain overseas: Chinese tomb culture and folklore have virtually no awareness overseas; may read as exotic novelty rather than horror May need to reframe as "supernatural mystery" in marketing to de-emphasize Chinese cultural specificity
  • Shallow gameplay, weak retention hooks: Hands-on experience confirms thin content; core loop is repetitive with no meaningful progression system or strategic depth Compare to Identity V: full character mechanics, seasonal system, deep skill curve — Supernatural Ops currently falls well short
  • Weak viral loop, hard cold start: Gameplay doesn't generate natural "highlight moments" or organic spread; heavy reliance on paid UA expected, with limited organic growth Expect above-average CAC, significant ROI pressure
