产品认知

Product Understanding

✅ 优势 / 机会点
✅ Strengths / Opportunities
  • 品类空白赛道：海外无直接竞品，轻恐+社交+搜打撤玩法尚未有成熟产品占据，先发可建立品类心智
  • 女性用户高占比：女性用户带来强力的社交裂变潜力，对用户获取有极大增益。同时该用户群付费意愿也较强
  • 轻恐氛围激发社交潜力：轻度恐怖的游戏氛围天然会激发用户的社交行为，促进用户联结和创造出内容传播
  • 玩法拓展潜力高：Steam 上多人组队爬山等玩法热度很高，超自然行动已能把类似玩法做成活动内容；后续若出现新热门玩法，也有机会快速接入游戏内，增加中后期内容丰富度和活跃爆发钩子
  • 本地化内容有潜力：研发商会有国内海外两条分支，有对海外做本地化内容优化的可行性，例如新增各地特色藏品、地标恐怖元素等
  • Category blue ocean: No direct overseas competitor; light-horror + social + extraction gameplay has no mature overseas leader yet, so first-mover can build category mindshare
  • High female user ratio: Female users bring strong social referral potential, materially improving user acquisition. This audience also tends to show stronger spending intent
  • Light horror drives social behavior: Low-intensity fear naturally encourages players to connect, react together, and generate shareable content moments
  • High gameplay expansion potential: Multiplayer climbing and co-op challenge trends on Steam show that hot social mechanics can be eventized. Supernatural Action has already added similar activity modes, giving it a path to refresh mid-late content and create activity spikes
  • Localized content potential: Separate domestic and overseas branches make overseas content adaptation feasible, such as local collectibles and landmark horror elements
⚠️ 核心问题（基于实际体验）
⚠️ Core Issues (Based on Hands-on Experience)
  • 角色中国脸 + 代入感问题：现有角色设计以中国人面孔为主，海外玩家——尤其是东南亚和拉美用户——代入感弱，影响情感连接
  • 盗墓/中式民俗题材接受度存疑：盗墓文化和中国民俗元素在海外几乎没有认知基础，可能被认为猎奇而非恐怖，题材吸引力难以判断
  • 内容解锁较慢：核心功能、内容和组队体验的释放节奏偏后，可能导致新用户在前期尚未感受到社交乐趣前就流失
  • 玩法深度偏浅，留存钩子不足：由于当前体验偏PVE，核心循环能否支撑长期策略深度仍待验证；亲自体验后感受明显：内容偏薄、重复性较高，缺乏支撑长线留存的成长体系
  • Chinese-face characters + immersion gap: Current characters are predominantly Chinese-featured; SEA and LATAM players struggle to identify with them, weakening emotional connection
  • Tomb-raider / Chinese folklore theme — uncertain overseas: Chinese tomb culture and folklore have virtually no awareness overseas; may read as exotic novelty rather than horror
  • Slow content unlock: Core features, content, and team-play moments unlock relatively late, creating early churn risk before new users feel the social fun
  • Shallow gameplay, weak retention hooks: Because the current experience is PvE-led, whether the core loop can support long-term strategic depth is still unproven. Hands-on play suggests thin content, high repetition, and weak progression hooks
